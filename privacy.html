Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

JamDay is designed so that your information stays with you. This policy explains what the app and this website do with data.

No account, no tracking

  • You don’t need an account to use JamDay.
  • JamDay collects no analytics, telemetry, crash reports or location data.
  • This website uses no cookies and no third-party trackers.

Where your data lives

Tasks, notes, events, folders, tags, attachments and settings are stored in a local database in your browser profile on your device. We have no access to it. Deleting the app’s site data, or using “Delete all data on this device” in Settings, removes it.

When JamDay uses the network

  • App updates: loading the app files from this website.
  • Google connection (optional): if you connect Google, JamDay talks directly to Google’s Calendar and Drive APIs using your own OAuth client. Access tokens are kept in memory only. Google Calendar events are held in memory and never written to the device database. Two-way sync only creates, edits and deletes events you created in JamDay. Drive sync stores one file in Drive’s hidden app-data folder.
  • AI assistant (optional, off by default): when you send a message, your message and the data you chose to share (tasks, calendar including Google events, notes) are sent directly to the provider you picked (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or xAI) using your API key. Their privacy policies apply to that data. On-device AI (Chrome’s built-in model) runs locally.
  • Import helpers: importing Excel or PDF files downloads a reader library from a public CDN (cdnjs). Your files are read on your device and are not uploaded.

Purchases

If you buy a license, the payment provider processes your payment details and email. We receive what we need to provide your license and support.
