Version 1.0 · November 13, 2026

JamDay 1.0 — first release

  • Tasks: Inbox, Today, Upcoming and Tasks views (list, Kanban, calendar); statuses, priorities, deadlines, flexible “week / month / someday” plans, subtasks, rich notes, people, attachments and Quick Look.
  • Plain-language dates and quick tokens: @ date, # tag, & folder, ! priority, + person.
  • Habits: any task can repeat, with streaks, a custom repeat editor and flame markers across the app.
  • Calendar: day, 2/3/4-day, week and month views with drag to create, move and resize; optional Google Calendar connection with two-way sync and Meet links; .ics import.
  • Notes: block editor with tables, images, callouts and code; slash menu that works mid-line; @ mentions and backlinks; covers and icons; Markdown export.
  • Folders & tags with their own pages, cards or list layouts, and grouping.
  • Quick Launcher (Alt+Space) with commands and quick actions, and a quick panel with dropdown, floating and docked styles.
  • AI: on-device rewrite and summarize; optional assistant for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok with Auto AI.
  • Import & export: JSON backups, CSV, Excel, Markdown, PDF and .ics; optional Google Drive sync.
  • Light, dark and glass themes, accent colours, sidebar customization, Trash, reminders, demo content and a feature tour.
